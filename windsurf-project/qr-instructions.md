## 🚀 **How It Works:**

### **For Customers:**
1. **Scan QR Code** with phone camera
2. **Automatically redirected** to your store
3. **Start shopping** immediately
4. **Place orders** from their phone

### **For You (Store Owner):**
1. **Print QR Code** on marketing materials
2. **Display at store** - counter, door, products
3. **Share online** - WhatsApp, social media
4. **Get more orders** from QR scans

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Setup:**

### **Step 1: Deploy QR Store**
1. **Upload `qr-store.html`** to your hosting
2. **Access URL:** `https://your-domain.com/qr-store.html`
3. **Test QR code** - should redirect to store

### **Step 2: Generate QR Code**
1. **Visit your QR store page**
2. **Right-click QR code** → Save image
3. **Print QR code** on materials

### **Step 3: Display QR Code**
**Places to put QR code:**
- 🏪 **Store Counter** - Customers scan while waiting

- 📦 **Product Shelves** - Scan specific products
- 📱 **Business Cards** - Include QR code
- 📧 **Delivery Bags** - Reorder easily
